## Reference-driven revision limits

- New guard-height, shoulder-relative movement, recovery hysteresis and onset-order buffering pass synthetic tests. They have not yet resolved the user-reported failure in a physical replay. Fast combinations whose extensions first appear in the same received frame are deliberately rejected as ambiguous; the current relay samples roughly every 80 ms, so real sampling limitations require measurement.
- Pose-only labeled trial recording is opt-in and local. Exported landmarks describe body movement and should be shared deliberately. Labels assert intended movements; evaluation does not prove boxing form or independent ground truth. An order-reversed pair can have two matching punch classes while its exact-trial result correctly fails.
- Monocular normalized z and elbow angles are estimates, not metric anatomy. Wrist-height rules can reject valid camera views or stance variations; no camera-specific threshold adjustment is justified until labeled trials are reviewed.

- Face-relative guard thresholds are provisional. Nose/shoulder visibility is required; absent or implausible face observations suppress recognition rather than providing shoulder-only fallback. A controlled hold is required for both arming and recovery, adding latency. Camera-estimated z is not calibrated anatomical depth. Physical accuracy across stance, yaw, face touching, lighting, and camera placement is NOT VERIFIED.
